# Activity 4 – Step 2: Add an edge case

**Chatbot / model:** Gemini 3.1Pro

## Prompt

```text
You will be given a list of foods delimited by three hashes. Extract all the fruits and write them out as a list.
###
bread, cheese, carrot, rice, chicken, tomato, strawberry yogurt, fruit cake
###
```

## Response

```text
* **Tomato** (botanically classified as a fruit)

> **Note:** While "strawberry yogurt" and "fruit cake" contain fruit or fruit flavoring, they are categorized as a dairy product and a baked good, respectively.
```
